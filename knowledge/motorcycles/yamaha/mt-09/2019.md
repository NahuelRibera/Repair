# Yamaha MT-09 2019

## Quick specifications

### Engine

- Configuration: Liquid-cooled, 4-stroke, DOHC, inline 3-cylinder.
- Displacement: 847 cm3.
- Starting system: electric starter.

### Dimensions and weight

- Overall length: 2075 mm.
- Overall width: 815 mm.
- Overall height: 1120 mm.
- Seat height: 820 mm.
- Wheelbase: 1440 mm.
- Ground clearance: 135 mm.
- Minimum turning radius: 3.0 m.
- Curb weight: 193 kg.
- Maximum permitted load: 174 kg including rider, passenger, cargo and accessories.

### Fuel

- Tank capacity: 14 L.
- Reserve amount: 2.8 L.
- Fuel: Premium unleaded gasoline; E10 is acceptable.

### Electrical system

- System voltage: 12 V.
- Battery: YTZ10S.
- Battery capacity: 12 V, 8.6 Ah (10 HR).
- Battery type: VRLA (Valve Regulated Lead Acid).

### Wheels, tires and brakes

- Front tire: 120/70 ZR17 M/C (58W), tubeless.
- Rear tire: 180/55 ZR17M/C (73W), tubeless.
- Front cold pressure: 250 kPa / 2.50 bar / 36 psi.
- Rear cold pressure: 290 kPa / 2.90 bar / 42 psi.
- Minimum tread depth: 1.0 mm front and rear.
- Front brake: hydraulic dual disc.
- Rear brake: hydraulic single disc.
- Brake fluid: DOT 4.
- Front suspension: telescopic fork.
- Rear suspension: swingarm with link suspension.

## Periodic maintenance

### Engine oil

**Interval:**
- First change: 1,000 km or 1 month.
- Then: every 6,000 km or 6 months.

**Accepted viscosity grades:** 10W-40, 10W-50, 15W-40, 20W-40 or 20W-50.

**Oil grade:** API SG or higher, JASO MA.

**Quantity:**
- Oil change only: 2.40 L.
- Oil and filter change: 2.70 L.

**Level check:**
- Check the engine-oil level regularly and after service.
- Keep the motorcycle upright on level ground.
- Warm the engine, switch it off and allow the oil to settle before reading the level.
- Keep the oil between the minimum and maximum marks.

### Oil filter and drain bolt

**Oil-filter replacement points:**
- 1,000 km or 1 month.
- 13,000 km or 12 months.
- 25,000 km or 24 months.
- Continue the repeating maintenance cycle afterward.

**Tightening torques:**
- Oil filter cartridge: 17 N·m.
- Engine oil drain bolt: 43 N·m.

**Service note:** use a new drain-bolt gasket when the oil is changed.

### Spark plugs

**Type:** NGK CPR9EA9.

**Gap:** 0.8-0.9 mm.

**Tightening torque:** 13 N·m.

**Maintenance:** Replace every 19,000 km or 18 months; inspect and clean at the scheduled services.

**Condition check:**
- Electrode erosion or heavy carbon/deposits indicates replacement is needed.
- A clearly different insulator color between cylinders can indicate abnormal engine operation.

### Air filter

**Replacement interval:** 37,000 km.

**Severe-use note:** replace more frequently after repeated riding in rain, dust or dirty conditions.

**Element type:** disposable oil-coated paper element; do not clean it with compressed air.



### Valve clearance

**Interval:** every 42,000 km.

**Condition:** check and adjust with the engine cold.

**Why it matters:** incorrect clearance can contribute to abnormal engine noise, poor combustion and eventually engine damage.

### Cooling system

**Coolant replacement:** Every 3 years.

**Capacity:**
- Reservoir to maximum mark: 0.25 L.
- Radiator and cooling circuit: 1.93 L.

**Checks:**
- Check coolant only when the engine is cold.
- Inspect hoses and the cooling system for leaks or damage.
- Correct a low level before continued riding.

### Brakes

**Brake fluid:** DOT 4.

**Maintenance:**
- Check front and rear brake operation, pad wear, fluid level and leakage.
- Change brake fluid every 2 years.
- Replace brake-system internal seals every 2 years.
- Replace brake hoses every 4 years or sooner if cracked, damaged or leaking.
- There should be no free play at the brake lever end.

**Diagnostic clue:** a soft or spongy brake lever can indicate air in the hydraulic system.

### Clutch

**Lever free play:** 10.0-15.0 mm.

**Checks:**
- Verify smooth operation.
- Inspect the cable and lubricate it when required.
- Adjust free play if outside specification.

### Throttle

**Free play:** 3.0-5.0 mm.

**Checks:**
- Check free play periodically.
- Make sure the grip turns smoothly and returns correctly.
- Lubricate the cable and grip housing when required.

### Drive chain

**Specified slack / measurement:** 5.0-15.0 mm.

**Routine:**
- Check slack, alignment and general condition.
- Adjust if outside specification.
- Lubricate with lubricant suitable for an O-ring chain.

**Cleaning and lubrication interval:** Every 1,000 km and after washing, rain or wet riding.

**Tightening torques:**
- Rear axle nut: 150 N·m.
- Chain-adjuster locknuts: 16 N·m.

### Wheels, steering and bearings

- Inspect wheels for cracks, deformation and damage.
- Check wheel bearings for play or rough rotation.
- Check steering bearings for looseness.
- Repack steering bearings with suitable grease at 19,000 km intervals.
- Inspect swingarm pivot bearings and repack with grease at 50,000 km intervals.

### Chassis, cables and pivots

- Check chassis fittings and fasteners for looseness.
- Lubricate brake, clutch and shift-pedal pivot points at scheduled services.
- Check and lubricate the sidestand pivot.
- Inspect front-fork and rear-shock operation and look for oil leakage.
- Inspect suspension-link operation.

### Fuel, intake and exhaust

- Inspect fuel hoses for cracks or damage.
- Inspect the crankcase breather system.
- Check fuel-injection synchronization at scheduled services.
- Inspect the exhaust system for leakage and loose fasteners.
- Replace damaged exhaust gaskets when required.

## Suspension baseline

### Front suspension

- Rebound damping: 11 clicks out from fully hard.
- Compression damping: 11 clicks out from fully hard.

### Rear suspension

- Spring preload: position 4 of 7.

**Adjustment rule:** do not force suspension adjusters beyond their effective range. Where both fork legs have equivalent adjustments, keep left and right settings matched.

## Pre-ride checks

Before riding, check:

1. Fuel level and visible fuel leakage.
2. Engine-oil level and oil leakage.
3. Coolant level and cooling-system leakage.
4. Front and rear brake operation, pad condition, fluid level and leakage.
5. Clutch operation and lever free play.
6. Throttle operation.
7. Drive-chain slack, lubrication and condition.
8. Tire pressure, tread and visible damage.
9. Wheels and wheel-bearing condition.
10. Brake and shift pedal movement.
11. Brake and clutch lever movement.
12. Sidestand operation.
13. Chassis fasteners.
14. Instruments, lights, signals and switches.

## Electrical and battery notes

- The VRLA battery does not require electrolyte top-ups.
- Keep battery terminals clean, tight and correctly connected.
- Use a charger suitable for VRLA batteries.
- During battery removal, disconnect the negative lead first.
- During installation, connect the positive lead first.
- For extended storage, keep the battery charged and check its state periodically.

## Model-specific systems

- D-MODE provides selectable engine-response characteristics.
- Traction control can reduce engine output when rear-wheel slip is detected.
- The quick shift system supports clutchless upshifts when operating conditions are suitable.
- ABS is fitted to the braking system.

## Basic troubleshooting

### Engine does not start

1. Check the fuel level.
2. Refill if necessary and try again.
3. Operate the starter and observe cranking speed.
4. If cranking is slow, check battery-terminal connections and state of charge.
5. If cranking is normal, inspect spark-plug condition and electrodes.
6. Dry wet electrodes and verify the plug gap; replace damaged or worn plugs.
7. If the engine still does not start, continue diagnosis with ignition, fuel and compression checks.

### Engine overheats

1. Stop and allow the engine and radiator to cool.
2. Never remove the radiator cap while the cooling system is hot or pressurized.
3. Once cool, check coolant in the reservoir and radiator.
4. If coolant is low, inspect the cooling system for leakage.
5. If no leak is found, add coolant and restart the engine.
6. If overheating returns, the cooling system requires further diagnosis.

### Oil-pressure warning remains on

- The oil-pressure warning should go out after the engine starts.
- If it remains on or flickers while the oil level is correct, stop the engine and investigate before continuing to ride.

### Fuse blows repeatedly

1. Switch the affected circuit and ignition off.
2. Replace the fuse with the specified rating only.
3. Retest the circuit.
4. If it blows again, diagnose the electrical fault.

### Starter turns slowly

- Check battery-terminal tightness.
- Recharge the battery with a VRLA-compatible charger.
- If slow cranking continues with a charged battery, inspect battery condition and the starting circuit.

### Brake lever feels soft or spongy

- Check the brake-fluid level and look for leakage.
- Do not continue normal riding if braking performance is reduced.
- Air in the hydraulic system requires bleeding and further brake-system inspection.
