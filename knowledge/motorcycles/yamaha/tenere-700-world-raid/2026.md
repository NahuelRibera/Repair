# Yamaha Ténéré 700 World Raid 2026

## Quick specifications

### Engine

- Configuration: liquid-cooled, 4-stroke, DOHC, 2-cylinder inline engine.
- Displacement: 689 cm³.
- Bore × stroke: 80.0 × 68.6 mm.
- Starting system: electric starter.

### Dimensions and weight

- Overall length: 2370 mm.
- Overall width: 935 mm.
- Overall height: 1495 mm.
- Seat height: 895 mm.
- Wheelbase: 1595 mm.
- Ground clearance: 240 mm.
- Minimum turning radius: 3.1 m.
- Curb weight: 220 kg.
- Maximum permitted load: 174 kg including rider, passenger, cargo and accessories.

### Fuel

- Tank capacity: 23 L.
- Reserve amount: 3.2 L.
- Fuel type: unleaded gasoline; E10 is acceptable.

### Electrical system

- System voltage: 12 V.
- Battery: YTZ7S (F).
- Battery capacity: 12 V, 6.0 Ah (10 HR).
- Battery type: VRLA (Valve Regulated Lead Acid).

### Wheels, tires and brakes

- Front tire: 90/90-21 M/C 54V M+S, tube type.
- Rear tire: 150/70 R18 M/C 70V M+S, tube type.
- Front cold pressure: 220 kPa / 2.20 bar / 32 psi.
- Rear cold pressure: 250 kPa / 2.50 bar / 36 psi.
- Minimum tread depth: 1.0 mm front and rear.
- Front brake: hydraulic dual disc.
- Rear brake: hydraulic single disc.
- Front suspension: telescopic fork.
- Rear suspension: swingarm with link suspension.

## Periodic maintenance

### Engine oil

**Interval:**
- First change: 1,000 km or 1 month.
- Then: every 6,000 km or 6 months.

**Accepted viscosity grades:** 10W-40, 10W-50, 15W-40, 20W-40 or 20W-50.

**Oil grade:** API SG or higher, JASO MA.

**Quantity:**
- Oil change only: 2.30 L.
- Oil and filter change: 2.60 L.

**Level check:**
1. Keep the motorcycle upright on level ground.
2. Warm the engine, switch it off and wait a few minutes.
3. Read the level through the inspection window.
4. Keep the oil between the minimum and maximum marks.

### Oil filter and drain bolt

**Oil filter replacement points:**
- 1,000 km or 1 month.
- 13,000 km or 12 months.
- 25,000 km or 24 months.
- Continue the same repeating maintenance cycle afterward.

**Tightening torques:**
- Oil filter cartridge: 17 N·m.
- Engine oil drain bolt: 43 N·m.

### Spark plugs

**Type:** LMAR8A-9.

**Gap:** 0.8–0.9 mm.

**Tightening torque:** 13 N·m.

**Maintenance pattern:**
- Inspect, clean and adjust at 7,000, 19,000 and 31,000 km.
- Replace at 13,000 and 25,000 km.
- From 37,000 km / 36 months, repeat the maintenance cycle beginning with the 13,000 km / 12 month schedule.

### Air filter

**Replacement interval:** every 19,000 km.

**More frequent replacement:** shorten the interval after frequent use in rain, dust or dirty conditions.

**Important:** the filter element is replaced rather than cleaned with compressed air.

**Air-filter check hose:** inspect and clean during scheduled maintenance.

### Valve clearance

**Interval:** every 42,000 km.

**Condition:** check and adjust with the engine cold.

### Cooling system

**Coolant replacement interval:** every 3 years.

**Capacity:**
- Reservoir to maximum mark: 0.25 L.
- Radiator and cooling circuit: 1.61 L.

**Routine checks:**
- Check coolant with the engine cold.
- Inspect the system for leakage.
- Correct low coolant before riding.

### Brakes

**Brake fluid:** DOT 4.

**Maintenance:**
- Inspect front and rear brake operation, fluid level and leakage.
- Replace brake fluid every 2 years.
- Replace brake-system internal seals every 2 years.
- Replace brake hoses every 4 years or sooner if damaged.
- Check pad wear indicators.

**Brake lever:** there should be no free play at the lever end.

**Diagnostic clue:** a soft or spongy lever can indicate air in the hydraulic circuit.

### Clutch

**Lever free play:** 5.0–10.0 mm.

**Checks:**
- Verify smooth operation.
- Inspect the cable.
- Adjust free play when outside specification.

### Throttle

- Check for smooth rotation and automatic return.
- If movement or return is abnormal, inspect the throttle-control system.

### Drive chain

**Slack measurement — distance A:** 43.0–48.0 mm.

**Checks:**
- Check slack before each ride.
- Check chain condition and adjuster alignment.

**Cleaning and lubrication:**
- Clean and lubricate at the scheduled interval.
- Lubricate after washing.
- Lubricate after rain or wet riding.
- Use O-ring-chain-compatible lubricant.

**Rear-wheel tightening torques:**
- Rear axle nut: 105 N·m.
- Chain-adjuster locknuts: 16 N·m.

### Wheels, spokes and bearings

- Inspect wheel damage and runout.
- Check spoke tightness.
- Balance the wheel after tire or tube replacement.
- Check front and rear wheel bearings for play or rough rotation.
- Check steering bearings for looseness or free play.

### Steering damper

- Check steering-damper operation during scheduled maintenance.
- Inspect it for oil leakage.
- If its action changes noticeably or leakage is present, inspect the unit before continued hard use.

### Chassis lubrication and fasteners

- Check and lubricate control cables and lever/pedal pivots.
- Lubricate the sidestand pivot.
- Lubricate rear-suspension and swingarm pivot points as scheduled.
- Check chassis fittings and fasteners for correct tightness.

## Suspension baseline

### Front fork

**Adjustments:** spring preload, rebound damping and compression damping.

**Standard settings:**
- Spring preload: distance A = 19.0 mm.
- Rebound damping: 13 clicks out from the fully hard position.
- Compression damping: 9 clicks out from the fully hard position.

### Rear shock

**Standard settings:**
- Spring preload: 10 clicks.
- Rebound damping: 11 clicks out from the fully hard position.
- Compression damping: 14 clicks out from the fully hard position.

### Fork-pressure note

Very rough riding can raise air pressure inside the fork and make the front suspension feel firmer. If necessary, bleed both fork legs with the front end unloaded.

**Adjustment rule:** always keep left and right fork settings equal.

## Electrical and battery notes

- Use only the specified battery type.
- The VRLA battery does not need electrolyte top-ups.
- Keep terminals clean and tight.
- Use a charger compatible with VRLA batteries.
- A non-specified battery can interfere with electronic systems.
- During removal, disconnect the negative terminal first.
- During installation, connect the positive terminal first.

## Electronic rider systems

### Power delivery

- Two power-delivery maps are available.
- Level 1 provides the sportier throttle response.
- Level 2 provides the milder response.

### Traction control

- Traction control regulates engine output when rear-wheel slip is detected.
- The system uses vehicle-motion information and adapts intervention to riding conditions.
- Traction control can be switched off when stopped when conditions require it, such as freeing the rear wheel in soft terrain.

### ABS

- ABS operates on the front and rear brakes.
- On loose or rough surfaces, stopping distance can be longer with ABS.
- ABS can be disabled for off-road use.
- Use ABS for paved-road riding.

### Cruise control and YVSL

- Cruise control is fitted.
- YVSL is a variable speed-limiter function.
- Cruise control and YVSL cannot be active at the same time.
- YVSL can store and resume a previously set speed limit while the system remains in standby.

### Quick shifter

- Quick shifter availability depends on equipment.
- Upshift and downshift functions can be configured separately.
- With the function disabled for one direction, use the clutch for shifts in that direction.
- Quick-shifter-assisted upshifts require sufficient vehicle and engine speed.

## Basic troubleshooting

### Engine does not start

1. Check fuel level.
2. Refill if necessary and retry.
3. Operate the starter and note cranking speed.
4. If cranking is slow, inspect battery connections and charge state.
5. If cranking is normal, inspect spark-plug electrodes.
6. Dry wet electrodes and verify plug gap; replace plugs if required.
7. If the engine still does not start, continue diagnosis with ignition, fuel and compression checks.

### Engine overheats

1. Stop and allow the engine and radiator to cool.
2. Never remove the radiator cap while the system is hot.
3. Check coolant in the reservoir and radiator once cool.
4. If the level is low, inspect for leakage.
5. If no leak is found, add coolant.
6. Restart and monitor temperature.
7. If overheating returns, inspect the cooling system further.

### Oil-pressure warning remains on

- The oil-pressure warning should go out after startup.
- If it flickers or remains on with the correct oil level, stop the engine immediately.

### Fuse blows repeatedly

1. Switch the relevant circuit and ignition off.
2. Replace the fuse only with the specified rating.
3. Retest the circuit.
4. If the fuse blows again, diagnose the electrical fault rather than installing a larger fuse.

### Slow starter operation

- Check battery-terminal tightness.
- Charge the battery with a VRLA-compatible charger.
- If slow cranking continues with a charged battery, inspect the battery and starting circuit.
