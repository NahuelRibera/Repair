# Yamaha MT-07 2024

## Quick specifications

### Engine

- Configuration: liquid-cooled, 4-stroke, DOHC, inline 2-cylinder.
- Displacement: 689 cm³.
- Bore × stroke: 80.0 × 68.6 mm.
- Starting system: electric starter.

### Dimensions and weight

- Overall length: 2085 mm.
- Overall width: 780 mm.
- Overall height: 1115 mm.
- Seat height: 805 mm.
- Wheelbase: 1400 mm.
- Ground clearance: 140 mm.
- Minimum turning radius: 2.7 m.
- Curb weight: 184 kg.
- Maximum permitted load: 171 kg including rider, passenger, cargo and accessories.

### Fuel

- Tank capacity: 14 L.
- Reserve amount: 2.7 L.
- Fuel: unleaded gasoline; E10 is acceptable.
- Minimum octane rating: RON 90.

### Electrical system

- System voltage: 12 V.
- Battery: YTZ10S.
- Battery capacity: 12 V, 8.6 Ah (10 HR).
- Battery type: VRLA (Valve Regulated Lead Acid).

### Wheels, tires and brakes

- Front tire: 120/70 ZR 17M/C (58W), tubeless.
- Rear tire: 180/55 ZR 17M/C (73W), tubeless.
- Front cold pressure: 225 kPa / 2.25 bar / 33 psi.
- Rear cold pressure: 250 kPa / 2.50 bar / 36 psi.
- Minimum tread depth: 1.0 mm front and rear.
- Front brake: hydraulic dual disc.
- Rear brake: hydraulic single disc.
- Brake fluid: DOT 4.
- Front suspension: telescopic fork.
- Rear suspension: swingarm with link suspension.

## Periodic maintenance

### Engine oil

**Interval:**
- First change: 1,000 km or 1 month.
- Then: every 6,000 km or 6 months.

**Accepted viscosity grades:** 10W-40, 10W-50, 15W-40, 20W-40 or 20W-50.

**Oil grade:** API SG or higher, JASO MA.

**Quantity:**
- Oil change only: 2.30 L.
- Oil and filter change: 2.60 L.

**Level check:**
1. Keep the motorcycle upright on level ground.
2. Warm the engine and switch it off.
3. Wait a few minutes for the oil to settle.
4. Read the oil level through the inspection window.
5. Keep the level between the minimum and maximum marks.

### Oil filter and drain bolt

**Oil-filter replacement points:**
- 1,000 km or 1 month.
- 13,000 km or 12 months.
- 25,000 km or 24 months.
- Continue the repeating maintenance cycle afterward.

**Tightening torques:**
- Oil filter cartridge: 17 N·m.
- Engine oil drain bolt: 43 N·m.

**Service note:** use a new drain-bolt gasket when the oil is changed.

### Spark plugs

**Type:** NGK LMAR8A-9.

**Gap:** 0.8-0.9 mm.

**Tightening torque:** 13 N·m.

**Maintenance pattern:**
- Inspect, clean and adjust at 7,000, 19,000 and 31,000 km.
- Replace at 13,000 and 25,000 km.
- From 37,000 km / 36 months, repeat the maintenance cycle beginning with the 13,000 km / 12 month schedule.

**Condition check:**
- Excessive deposits or electrode erosion indicate that replacement is needed.
- A clearly different insulator color between cylinders can indicate abnormal engine operation.

### Air filter

**Replacement interval:** every 37,000 km.

**Element type:** disposable oil-coated paper element.

**Important:** do not clean the element with compressed air.

**Severe-use note:** replace more frequently after repeated riding in rain, dust or dirty conditions.

**Air-filter check hose:** inspect and clean at scheduled services.

### Valve clearance

**Interval:** every 42,000 km.

**Condition:** check and adjust with the engine cold.

**Why it matters:** incorrect valve clearance can contribute to abnormal engine noise, poor combustion and eventually engine damage.

### Cooling system

**Coolant replacement:** 25,000 km or 24 months; continue with the repeating maintenance cycle afterward.

**Capacity:**
- Reservoir to maximum mark: 0.25 L.
- Radiator and cooling circuit: 1.60 L.

**Checks:**
- Check the coolant level with the engine cold.
- Inspect the cooling system for leakage and damaged hoses.
- Do not open the radiator cap while the cooling system is hot or pressurized.

### Brakes

**Brake fluid:** DOT 4.

**Maintenance:**
- Check front and rear brake operation, pad wear, fluid level and leakage.
- Change brake fluid every 2 years.
- Replace internal hydraulic components in the master cylinders and calipers every 2 years.
- Replace brake hoses every 4 years or sooner if cracked or damaged.
- There should be no free play at the brake lever end.

**Diagnostic clue:** a soft or spongy brake lever can indicate air in the hydraulic system.

### Clutch

**Lever free play:** 5.0-10.0 mm.

**Checks:**
- Verify smooth operation.
- Inspect the clutch cable and lubricate it when required.
- Adjust free play when outside specification.

### Throttle

**Free play:** 3.0-5.0 mm.

**Idle speed:** 1,250-1,450 r/min.

**Checks:**
- Check free play periodically.
- Make sure the throttle rotates smoothly and returns correctly.
- Lubricate the cable and grip housing when required.

### Drive chain

**Slack measurement — distance A:** 51.0-56.0 mm.

**Maximum warning point:** if distance A exceeds 58.0 mm, the chain can contact and damage surrounding chassis components.

**Routine:**
- Check slack before each ride.
- Check chain condition and wheel alignment.
- Adjust when outside specification.
- Use lubricant suitable for an O-ring chain.

**Cleaning and lubrication:** every 1,000 km and after washing, riding in rain or riding in wet areas.

**Tightening torques:**
- Rear axle nut: 105 N·m.
- Chain-adjuster locknuts: 16 N·m.

### Wheels, steering and bearings

- Inspect cast wheels for cracks, bends, warpage and other damage.
- Balance a wheel whenever the tire or wheel is changed.
- Check wheel bearings for play or rough rotation.
- Check steering bearings for looseness.
- Repack steering bearings with suitable grease every 19,000 km.
- Inspect swingarm pivot bearings and repack them with lithium-soap-based grease every 50,000 km.

### Chassis, cables and pivots

- Check chassis fittings and fasteners for looseness.
- Lubricate brake, clutch and shift-pedal pivot points at scheduled services.
- Check and lubricate the sidestand pivot.
- Inspect front-fork operation and look for scratches, damage and oil leakage.
- Inspect rear-shock operation and oil leakage.
- Check rear-suspension linkage operation.

### Fuel, intake and exhaust

- Inspect fuel hoses for cracks or damage.
- Inspect the crankcase breather system.
- Check engine idle speed at scheduled services.
- Check fuel-injection synchronization at scheduled services.
- Inspect the exhaust system for leaks and loose fasteners.
- Replace damaged exhaust gaskets when required.

## Suspension baseline

### Front suspension

- No routine external front-fork adjustment is specified.
- Inspect fork tubes for scratches, damage and oil leakage.
- Check that the fork compresses and rebounds smoothly.

### Rear suspension

- Spring preload: standard position 4 of 7.
- Rebound damping: 1.5 turns out from fully hard.

**Adjustment rule:** do not force the rebound adjuster beyond its effective range.

- The VRLA battery does not require electrolyte top-ups.
- Keep the battery terminals clean, tight and correctly connected.
- Use a charger suitable for VRLA batteries.
- During battery removal, disconnect the negative lead first.
- During installation, connect the positive lead first.
- For extended storage, keep the battery charged and check its state periodically.

## Basic troubleshooting

### Engine does not start

1. Check the fuel level.
2. Refill if necessary and try again.
3. Operate the starter and observe cranking speed.
4. If cranking is slow, check battery-terminal connections and state of charge.
5. If cranking is normal, inspect spark-plug condition and electrodes.
6. Dry wet electrodes and verify the plug gap; replace damaged or worn plugs.
7. If the engine still does not start, continue diagnosis with ignition, fuel and compression checks.

### Engine overheats

1. Stop and allow the engine and radiator to cool.
2. Never remove the radiator cap while the cooling system is hot or pressurized.
3. Once cool, check coolant in the reservoir and radiator.
4. If coolant is low, inspect the cooling system for leakage.
5. If no leak is found, add coolant and restart the engine.
6. If overheating returns, the cooling system requires further diagnosis.

### Oil-pressure warning remains on

- The oil-pressure warning should go out after the engine starts.
- If it remains on or flickers while the oil level is correct, stop the engine and investigate before continuing to ride.

### Fuse blows repeatedly

1. Switch the affected circuit and ignition off.
2. Replace the fuse with the specified rating only.
3. Retest the circuit.
4. If it blows again, diagnose the electrical fault.

### Starter turns slowly

- Check battery-terminal tightness.
- Recharge the battery with a VRLA-compatible charger.
- If slow cranking continues with a charged battery, inspect battery condition and the starting circuit.

### Brake lever feels soft or spongy

- Check the brake-fluid level and look for leakage.
- Do not continue normal riding if braking performance is reduced.
- Air in the hydraulic system requires bleeding and further brake-system inspection.

### Drive chain is noisy or visibly loose

- Check distance A and adjust it to 51.0-56.0 mm.
- Check chain lubrication and condition.
- Verify that both chain-adjuster plates are aligned.
- If the chain has tight spots, damage or excessive wear, inspect the chain and sprockets as a set.
